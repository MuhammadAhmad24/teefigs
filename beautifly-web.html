Beautifly — Wedding Cinematography Website

A concept one-page website designed for a wedding cinematography and photography studio. The design creates an elegant, emotional and editorial online presence that helps couples explore featured films, understand the studio’s story and easily get in touch.

Role: UX/UI Designer

Platform: One-page Website

Industry: Wedding Film & Photography

Wedding Filmographers Website
Project Overview

Elegant website design for a wedding storytelling brand.

Overview

This website was designed to present a wedding filmography brand in a premium, emotional and visually refined way. The full-page screenshot highlights the complete website structure, including the hero section, featured work, about content and contact form.

Design Direction

The design uses a soft neutral background, elegant typography, rounded image blocks, balanced spacing and a warm wedding-focused visual style. The overall feel is calm, cinematic and editorial.

Key Features

  • Large hero image area for strong first impression
  • Featured work section inside the website design
  • About section to build trust and explain the brand
  • Contact section with enquiry form
  • Clean footer and social media area
